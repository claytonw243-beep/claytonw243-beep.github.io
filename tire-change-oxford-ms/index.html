Signs you need a tire change

  • A tire that's visibly flat or losing air fast
  • A loud bang or thump followed by the car pulling to one side
  • A tire that looks damaged, torn, or separated from the rim

What we do when we arrive

  1. Confirm you have a usable spare, jack, and lug wrench
  2. Safely jack up the car and remove the damaged tire
  3. Mount your spare and torque the lug nuts properly
  4. Let you know if your spare is low or needs air before you drive on it

If it happens on the highway

A flat tire on the shoulder of Highway 6 or Highway 7 is more stressful than one in a parking lot. Turn your hazards on, get as far onto the shoulder as you safely can, and call us with your direction of travel and nearest mile marker or exit. See ourHighway Corridor pagefor more on staying safe while you wait.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you carry new tires?

No — we swap your flat for your own spare tire. We don't sell or stock replacement tires. If your spare is also flat or missing, call us anyway and we'll be honest about whether we can help or whether you need a tow.

How much does a tire change cost?

A tire change is $100. See our Pricing & How It Works page for more on what's included.

What if I don't have a spare tire?

Without a spare or a repairable tire, a roadside swap isn't possible. Call us and we'll tell you straight whether it's something we can help with or whether you'll need a tow.

Can you patch or plug a tire instead of swapping it?

We come prepared to swap in your spare, not to patch or plug. If your tire just needs a quick fix rather than a full swap, tell us when you call and we'll let you know what we can do.
